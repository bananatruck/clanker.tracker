# Cast — Clankerdom Deliverance

Character sheets. Names and roles are authored in [`raw-inputs.md`](./raw-inputs.md); this file is the production sheet for art and animation only.

---

## Sir Khums Alaude — "Kh. Laude"

**Role:** The player. The knight.
**Source:** [1] `raw-inputs.md` §7
**Description:** A knight of no particular distinction. Excellent handwriting, urgent need for dental coverage. Takes the commission because the commission comes with a stipend.
**Sprite:** Tiny Swords → `Factions/Knights/Troops/Warrior` (blue ribbon, free pack)
**Anims:** idle 4f · walk 6f · attack 6f · victory 4f
**Notes:** Armour accrues a sponsor logo from tier Devastator onward.

---

## King Net And Yahoo

**Role:** Declares Clankerdom Deliverance from atop the Tower. Never descends. Never fights.
**Source:** §7
**Description:** King of Clankerdom. Lives at the top of a tower so tall its shadow has its own timezone.
**Sprite:** original — custom silhouette in a tower window; the Tower itself from Tiny Swords → `Buildings/Castle`, scaled and stacked
**Anims:** none required — he is never seen at ground level. Appears only as a lit window, a banner, and dispatch text.

## The Chilled Rens

## The Chud Lord of Unemployment — "Reddit D Mod"

**Role:** Cast as the arch-villain. Was the last line of defence between the King's army and the elf-villages.
**Source:** §2 (`raw-inputs.md`), §7
**Sprite:** 8-Directional Knight (Hormelz, CC0) or Holy Knights Sprite Pack, recolored dark
**Anims:** idle 4f · guard 4f · speak 2f · wave 4f
**Notes:** The wave at the ending is the single most important frame in the game. It is not an attack animation.
